## Print Media Guidelines

### Before Publishing

- [ ] Legal review completed
- [ ] Risk disclaimers included
- [ ] No misleading claims
- [ ] Contact information accurate
- [ ] Geographic restrictions checked

### Content Requirements

- [ ] Risk warning prominent
- [ ] "Past performance not indicative of future results" included
- [ ] No guaranteed returns
- [ ] Testimonials with disclaimers
- [ ] Company identification clear

### Tone

- Professional, not sensational
- Educational, not promotional
- Transparent about limitations
- Evidence-based

### Visual Guidelines

- Clean, professional design
- No luxury lifestyle imagery
- No "get rich quick" visuals
- Trustworthy colors (blues, grays)
- Risk warning visible

### Distribution

- Target appropriate publications
- Respect editorial guidelines
- Provide high-resolution materials
- Include media kit
