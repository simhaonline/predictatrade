## Brand Photography Style

**If using real photography:**

- **Setting:** Modern office, clean desk, professional environment
- **People:** Diverse, professional, focused (not excited/emotional)
- **Lighting:** Clean, professional, not dramatic
- **Colors:** Blue (#1a365d) and gold (#d4af37) accents in environment
- **Mood:** Serious, focused, professional (not party/lifestyle)
- **Avoid:** Luxury cars, mansions, champagne, money stacks

## Image Generation Tips

### For Midjourney/DALL-E/Stable Diffusion:

1. **Use specific dimensions:**
   - Instagram: 1080x1080, 1080x1920
   - Facebook: 1200x630, 820x312
   - Twitter: 1200x675, 1500x500
   - LinkedIn: 1200x627
   - YouTube: 1280x720, 1920x1080

2. **Specify style:**
   - "Professional fintech"
   - "Clean, modern"
   - "Photorealistic" or "Illustration" or "Infographic"

3. **Mention colors:**
   - "Deep blue (#1a365d) and gold (#d4af37) color scheme"

4. **Avoid in prompts:**
   - "Get rich quick" imagery
   - Luxury lifestyle (cars, mansions)
   - Excessive money symbols
   - "100%" or "guaranteed" text

5. **Include technical specs:**
   - "No text in image" (if you'll add text later)
   - "White background" or "Transparent background"
   - "High resolution, 4K" for print
