- **Consent** must be free, specific, informed, unconditional and unambiguous, with a clear notice; consent for a specified purpose only.
- **Breach:** intimation to the Board and affected data principals without delay, with a detailed report within **72 hours**. (Separately, CERT-In requires reporting of certain cyber incidents within **6 hours**.)
- **Significant Data Fiduciaries** must appoint a DPO based in India, appoint an independent data auditor, run annual audits and undertake DPIAs.
- **Penalties:** up to **₹250 crore** for failure to take reasonable security safeguards and up to **₹200 crore** for failure to notify a breach, per instance.
- Applies extraterritorially where processing relates to offering goods or services to data principals in India.

**Singapore —** *Personal Data Protection Act 2012*, administered by the **Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC)**.

- **Consent obligation** and a **notification obligation**; a **Data Protection Officer** must be appointed and their business contact information made publicly available.
- Rights of **access and correction**, and **withdrawal of consent** with reasonable notice; **data portability** under the PDPA's data-portability obligation for certain organisations.
- Transfer Limitation Obligation: transferred data must receive protection comparable to the PDPA.
- Breach notification to the PDPC (and affected individuals) where the breach is notifiable — as a guide, within **3 calendar days** of assessing it as notifiable.
- Penalties can reach the higher of **SGD 1 million** or 10% of annual turnover for larger organisations.

**Australia —** *Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)* and the **Australian Privacy Principles**, administered by the **OAIC**.

- Rights of **access and correction**, **complaint to the OAIC**, and **erasure** on request in many circumstances.
- **Notifiable Data Breaches scheme** — notify the OAIC and affected individuals where a breach is likely to result in serious harm.
- **Reform underway under the *Privacy and Other Legislation Amendment Act 2024*:** the OAIC's strengthened enforcement and tiered penalties apply now (top-tier civil penalties can reach **AUD 50 million**); a **statutory tort for serious invasions of privacy** has been available since **10 June 2025**; **automated decision-making transparency in privacy policies** is required from **December 2026** (see §6.4); and a **Children's Online Privacy Code** is to be registered by **10 December 2026**.
- Overseas transfer: APP 8 — we remain accountable for the handling of your information by overseas recipients.

**Japan —** *Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI)*, administered by the **Personal Information Protection Commission (PPC)**. Rights of disclosure, correction, suspension of use and erasure; a **retention-period and purpose-limitation** regime; rules on providing personal data to third parties overseas with consent or an equivalent-standard recipient.

**Republic of Korea —** *Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA)*, administered by the **Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC)**. Strong rights of access, correction, deletion and suspension; **consent requirements for overseas transfer**; a **domestic agent** obligation for foreign providers meeting thresholds.

**Hong Kong SAR —** *Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486)*, administered by the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data (PCPD)**. Rights of **access and correction** under Data Protection Principles 6 and DPP1 (purpose and manner of collection); enforcement notices and penalties; transfer restrictions under section 33 where commenced `[VERIFY]`.

**Malaysia —** *Personal Data Protection Act 2010* (as amended), administered by the **Personal Data Protection Department (JPDP)**. Rights of access, correction, withdrawal of consent and prevention of processing likely to cause damage or distress; a cross-border transfer regime based on an adequate-protection or consent basis; the **Personal Data Protection (Amendment) Act** introduced mandatory breach notification and data-portability obligations `[CONFIRM COMMENCEMENT DATES]`.

**Indonesia —** *Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection*, administered by the **Ministry of Communication and Digital Affairs / the PDP Agency**. Rights of access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent and **data portability**; a **DPO** obligation for certain controllers; **72-hour** breach notification to the authority and affected subjects; cross-border transfer requires an adequate level of protection or consent; administrative fines up to **2% of annual revenue**.

**Thailand —** *Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019)*, administered by the **Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC)**. Rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent; a **72-hour** breach notification to the PDPC Office; cross-border transfer on an adequacy or appropriate-safeguards basis.

**Vietnam —** *Law No. 91/2025/QH15 on Personal Data Protection*, in force **1 January 2026**, together with **Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP** (also effective 1 January 2026, replacing Decree 13/2023), administered by the **Ministry of Public Security**.

- Rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, **withdrawal of consent** and complaint.

**Mainland China —** *Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)*, the *Data Security Law* and the *Cybersecurity Law*, administered by the **Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC)** and other regulators.

**New Zealand (for completeness) —** the *Privacy Act 2020* and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner; rights of access, correction and complaint, and IPP 12 cross-border rules.

#### Canada

**Law:** *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)*, administered by the **Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC)**; **Quebec's Law 25** (formerly Bill 64), administered by the **Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI)**; and substantially similar provincial laws in Alberta, British Columbia and elsewhere.

- **Ten legal bases**, including consent, contract, legal obligation, and legitimate interest.

- **Consumer Defence Code (Law 8.078/1990)** also applies, including the 7-day right of repentance for distance contracts.

#### Mexico

- **Privacy notices** must be precise: the data collected, each specific purpose, consent-based versus non-consent-based processing, and a sensitive-data classification. This policy, together with the notice shown at the point of collection, is intended to satisfy that requirement.
- **Processors are now directly regulated**, not only controllers.
- Penalties can reach substantial multiples of the minimum wage unit (UMA), doubled for sensitive data and repeat offences.
