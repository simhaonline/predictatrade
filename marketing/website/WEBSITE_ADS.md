> 
> Never trade with money you need for essential expenses. Trading is speculative and involves risk of loss.
>

> 
> Historical data and backtested results have inherent limitations. Actual results may differ materially from historical or backtested performance.

>

> Predict-A-Trade operates in compliance with applicable regulations. However, trading regulations vary by jurisdiction. Users are responsible for ensuring they comply with local laws.

## Website Compliance Checklist

### Legal Requirements

- [ ] Risk warning visible on all pages
- [ ] Full risk disclosure on signup page
- [ ] Terms of service accessible
- [ ] Privacy policy accessible
- [ ] Cookie consent (if applicable)
- [ ] Geographic restrictions enforced

### Content Requirements

- [ ] No guaranteed returns claims
- [ ] "Past performance not indicative of future results" on all trading content
- [ ] "Demo results may differ from live trading" on demo pages
- [ ] Testimonials with disclaimers
- [ ] Contact information visible

### Technical Requirements

- [ ] Mobile responsive
- [ ] Fast loading
- [ ] SSL certificate (HTTPS)
- [ ] Analytics tracking (with consent)
- [ ] Opt-out mechanism for emails

### Advertising Compliance

- [ ] Ad landing pages match ad claims
- [ ] Risk warnings on all ad landing pages
- [ ] No misleading ad copy
- [ ] Geographic targeting respects restrictions
- [ ] Age targeting appropriate (18+)

---

## Website Best Practices

### Above the Fold

- Clear value proposition
- Risk warning visible
- CTA button prominent
- No clutter

### Navigation

- Simple, intuitive
- Risk disclosure easy to find
- Contact information visible
- Login/signup clear

### Content

- Educational, not promotional
- Transparent about limitations
- Evidence-based claims
- Regular updates

### Trust Signals

- Regulatory compliance
- Transparent performance
- Customer testimonials (with disclaimers)
- Clear contact information

### Conversion Optimization

- Clear CTA
- Minimal form fields
- Demo before signup
- Risk warning before commitment
