## TV Interview Talking Points

### When Asked About Predict-A-Trade

**Key Messages:**

1. "We provide tools for smarter trading decisions — not guaranteed profits."

2. "Our platform combines AI analysis with proper risk management. We believe risk management is more important than entry signals."

3. "We're transparent about results — including losses. We believe transparency builds trust."

4. "We offer four distinct strategies because there's no one-size-fits-all approach to trading."

5. "We encourage everyone to try the demo account first. No real money at risk while you learn."

**Avoid:**
- Specific return percentages
- Guarantees of any kind
- "AI predicts the market" language
- Lifestyle marketing (cars, houses, luxury)
- Dismissing risks

---

### When Asked About AI in Trading

**Key Messages:**

1. "AI doesn't predict the market — it analyzes probability distributions."

2. "Our AI identifies patterns across multiple timeframes and market conditions. It's a tool, not a crystal ball."

3. "AI can process data faster than humans, but it can't eliminate risk. That's why we pair AI with risk management."

4. "The value of AI isn't accuracy — it's consistency and speed of analysis."

**Avoid:**
- "AI that always wins"
- "Secret algorithm" language
- Overpromising AI capabilities

---

### When Asked About Risks

**Key Messages:**

1. "Trading leveraged products like CFDs involves substantial risk. You can lose more than your deposit."

2. "Past performance is not indicative of future results. Historical data doesn't guarantee future outcomes."

3. "We always recommend trading with money you can afford to lose. Never risk essential funds."

4. "Our platform enforces risk management automatically because we know emotions can override logic."

**Avoid:**
- Minimizing risks
- "Low risk" claims
- Comparing to "safe" investments

---

### When Asked About Competition

**Key Messages:**

1. "We differentiate through transparency and education. Many platforms hide losing trades — we show them all."

2. "We offer four distinct strategies, not a one-size-fits-all approach."

3. "We encourage users to try the demo first. We're confident in our platform, but we want users to experience it before committing."

**Avoid:**
- Direct competitor criticism
- "We're better" claims
- Disparaging other platforms

---

### When Asked About Performance

**Key Messages:**

1. "We provide transparent performance tracking — all wins and losses are visible."

2. "Results vary by user, strategy, market conditions, and risk management. No two traders have identical results."

3. "Past performance is not indicative of future results. We're careful not to imply otherwise."

4. "We focus on process over outcome. Good risk management leads to long-term survival, even during losing streaks."

**Avoid:**
- Specific return percentages
- "Our users make X% returns"
- Cherry-picked winning examples

## TV Interview Preparation

### Before the Interview

- [ ] Research the show's audience and tone
- [ ] Prepare 3 key messages
- [ ] Practice answers to tough questions
- [ ] Review current market conditions
- [ ] Prepare visual aids (if applicable)
- [ ] Test technology (if remote)

### During the Interview

- Stay on message
- Be honest about limitations
- Use concrete examples
- Acknowledge risks
- Be personable but professional

### After the Interview

- Monitor social media reaction
- Prepare follow-up content
- Thank the host/show
- Document lessons learned

## TV Advertising Compliance

### Pre-Approval Checklist

- [ ] Risk warning visible throughout ad
- [ ] No guaranteed returns claims
- [ ] "Demo" clearly labeled
- [ ] Compliance review completed
- [ ] Legal approval obtained
- [ ] Geographic restrictions checked
- [ ] Time slot appropriate (no late-night get-rich-quick association)

### Content Restrictions

- [ ] No lifestyle marketing (luxury cars, houses)
- [ ] No "get rich quick" imagery
- [ ] No testimonials without disclaimers
- [ ] No specific return percentages
- [ ] No risk-free claims

### Disclosure Requirements

- [ ] Risk warning audible AND visible
- [ ] "Past performance not indicative of future results" included
- [ ] "Demo results may differ from live trading" included
- [ ] Company identification clear
- [ ] Contact information provided

### Time Restrictions

- [ ] No ads during children's programming
- [ ] No ads during sensitive programming (news tragedies, etc.)
- [ ] Appropriate time slots for financial products
