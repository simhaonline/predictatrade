## Video Production Guidelines

### Style Guide

**Visual Style:**
- Clean, modern, professional
- Blue (#1a365d) and gold (#d4af37) color scheme
- No flashy effects or transitions
- Smooth, professional animations
- Clear text overlays

**Tone:**
- Educational, not promotional
- Honest, not hype-driven
- Professional, not casual
- Transparent about limitations

**Music:**
- Professional, modern background music
- Not too energetic (not "pump up" music)
- Subtle, not distracting
- Royalty-free or licensed

**Voiceover:**
- Professional, clear voice
- Measured pace (not too fast)
- Confident but not arrogant
- Honest tone

### Technical Requirements

**Resolution:**
- YouTube: 1920x1080 (1080p) minimum, 3840x2160 (4K) preferred
- Social Media: 1080x1080 (square) or 1920x1080 (landscape)
- Instagram Reels/TikTok: 1080x1920 (vertical)

**Frame Rate:**
- Standard: 30fps
- Smooth animations: 60fps

**Audio:**
- Clear voiceover
- Background music (subtle)
- No distracting sound effects

### Legal Requirements

**Every Video Must Include:**
- Risk warning (audible and/or visible)
- "Past performance is not indicative of future results"
- "Trading involves substantial risk of loss"
- "Demo results may differ from live trading" (if showing demo)

**Prohibited in Videos:**
- Guaranteed returns claims
- Get rich quick imagery
- Luxury lifestyle marketing
- Specific income claims
- Risk-free claims

### Video Length Guidelines

**Social Media:**
- Instagram Reels: 15-30 seconds
- TikTok: 15-60 seconds
- Twitter/X: 30-60 seconds
- Facebook: 1-3 minutes
- YouTube Shorts: 15-60 seconds

**YouTube:**
- Tutorials: 5-15 minutes
- Deep dives: 10-20 minutes
- Market updates: 2-5 minutes

**Television:**
- Standard spot: 30 seconds
- Extended spot: 60 seconds
