Price is the market's emotion, spelled out in candles.

A chart is not a photograph of supply and demand. It's a diary of collective feeling. Every wick is a moment of fear. Every breakout is a surge of hope. Predict-A-Trade translates that mood into something you can act on — before the crowd realizes how it feels.

When gold spikes on a headline, the move isn't "fundamentals" — it's millions of hearts racing at once. The body of the candle is the consensus; the wick is the panic that got rejected.

Most traders read charts as if the market were a machine. It isn't. It's a crowd — anxious, euphoric, stubborn, and above all, predictable in its extremes. We measure those extremes so you can stand where the crowd loses its nerve.
