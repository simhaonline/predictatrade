⚠️ CRITICAL RISK WARNING

Trading leveraged products such as CFDs on gold (XAUUSD) involves substantial risk of loss. You could lose more than your initial investment. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

1. General Risk Warning

Trading on financial markets involves significant risks, including:

  • Market Risk: Prices can fluctuate rapidly and unpredictably
  • Leverage Risk: Amplifies both gains and losses
  • Liquidity Risk: Difficulty in closing positions at desired prices
  • Volatility Risk: Gold can experience extreme price swings
  • Gap Risk: Prices can gap over stop levels

2. CFD Trading Risks

Contracts for Difference (CFDs) are complex instruments. Consider the following:

  • CFDs are leveraged products - losses can exceed deposits
  • You do not own the underlying asset
  • Spreads and commissions affect profitability
  • Swap fees apply for overnight positions
  • Margin calls may require additional funds

Studies show that 74-89% of retail accounts lose money when trading CFDs.

3. XAUUSD (Gold) Specific Risks

Gold trading has unique characteristics:

  • High volatility, especially during economic uncertainty
  • Influenced by geopolitical events, interest rates, and USD strength
  • Thin liquidity during off-hours can widen spreads
  • Flash crashes and price spikes can occur
  • Physical delivery considerations for large positions

4. Algorithmic Trading Risks

Predict-A-Trade uses algorithmic systems. Be aware:

  • Algorithms can fail or produce unexpected results
  • Past algorithm performance does not guarantee future results
  • System outages or connectivity issues can affect execution
  • Market conditions can invalidate algorithm assumptions
  • Slippage may differ from backtested results

5. Demo vs. Live Trading

Demo accounts do NOT replicate all aspects of live trading:

  • Execution speeds and slippage may differ
  • Spreads may be tighter in demo
  • No real market impact from your trades
  • Emotional factors are absent in demo
  • Liquidity conditions may differ

Do not assume demo performance will translate to live results.

6. Signal Service Risks

Trading signals are NOT financial advice:

  • Signals are based on historical patterns and algorithms
  • No signal is guaranteed to be profitable
  • Market conditions can change rapidly
  • Execution quality depends on your broker
  • You are responsible for your own trading decisions

7. Financial Considerations

Before trading, consider:

  • Only trade with money you can afford to lose
  • Do not use borrowed money or essential funds
  • Consider your financial situation and objectives
  • Understand all costs involved (spreads, commissions, swaps)
  • Have sufficient margin to support positions

8. Psychological Risks

Trading can be emotionally challenging:

  • Fear and greed can impair decision-making
  • Overtrading is a common mistake
  • Revenge trading after losses can be destructive
  • Stress and anxiety can affect judgment
  • Addictive behaviors may develop

9. Regulatory Considerations

Trading regulations vary by jurisdiction:

  • Ensure trading is legal in your country
  • Understand tax implications in your jurisdiction
  • Use regulated brokers when possible
  • Be aware of consumer protection laws
