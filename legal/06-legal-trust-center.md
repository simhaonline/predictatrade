**We are not regulated as a financial services provider, anywhere.**

**You are responsible for determining whether using the Service is lawful where you are.** We restrict access from jurisdictions where distribution would create unacceptable regulatory risk — see [Terms & Policies](https://predictatrade.com/legal/terms-and-policies/) §4.3.
