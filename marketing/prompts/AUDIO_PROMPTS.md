## Audio Production Guidelines

### Voice Quality

**Requirements:**
- Professional recording quality
- Clear pronunciation
- Measured pace (not too fast)
- Confident but not arrogant
- Honest, not salesy

**Avoid:**
- Too energetic (sounds like infomercial)
- Too monotone (sounds boring)
- Too fast (hard to understand)
- Too casual (sounds unprofessional)

### Audio Specifications

**Format:**
- WAV or FLAC for production
- MP3 for distribution (320kbps minimum)
- Sample rate: 44.1kHz or 48kHz
- Bit depth: 16-bit or 24-bit

**Levels:**
- Voice: -12dB to -6dB average
- Music: -18dB to -12dB (under voice)
- No clipping

### Legal Requirements

**Every Audio Must Include:**
- Risk warning (audible)
- "Past performance is not indicative of future results"
- "Trading involves substantial risk of loss"

**Prohibited in Audio:**
- Guaranteed returns claims
- Get rich quick language
- Specific income claims
- Risk-free claims
