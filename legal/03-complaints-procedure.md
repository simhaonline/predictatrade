## 1. Our commitment

1.1 We take complaints seriously. We will handle yours **fairly, promptly, consistently and free of charge**.

1.2 We will not treat you less favourably, withdraw the Service, or degrade your access because you complained. Retaliation for complaining is itself a breach of this procedure.

1.3 **Complaining to us does not limit your other rights.** You may complain to a regulator, an ombudsman, a consumer-protection body, or a court at any time — before, during or after our process. See §6.

1.4 This procedure applies to complaints about the Service, our conduct, our staff, our billing, our handling of your personal data, or the way we have treated you.

## 2. How to make a complaint

### 2.1 What to send

Email **[COMPLAINTS EMAIL]** with **"Complaint"** in the subject line. Include as much of the following as you can:

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Your name** | As it appears on your account |
| **Contact details** | Email address; telephone if you prefer to be called |
| **Account reference** | Username or subscription ID, if applicable |

- **Never** include passwords, one-time codes, API keys, full payment card numbers, CVV or bank login details. We will never ask for them. If you have already sent them, change them immediately and tell us.
- Do not send special-category or sensitive data (health, biometric, religious or similar) unless it is directly relevant to the complaint.

## 3. What happens next

### 3.1 Acknowledgement

We acknowledge **within 3 business days** of receipt, giving you:

- your **complaint reference**;
- the name of the person handling it;
- when you can expect a substantive response; and
- a copy of this procedure.

### 3.2 Assessment

We decide who is best placed to investigate — billing, engineering, data protection, or senior management. **Data-protection complaints** are routed to the Data Protection Officer or privacy lead (see Privacy Policy §14) and are handled under the deadlines in §4.

### 3.3 Investigation

We review the relevant records, speak to the people involved, and may contact you for clarification. We keep a written record of the investigation.

### 3.4 Response

We send a **written final response** explaining:

- our understanding of your complaint;
- what we found;
- our decision and the reasons for it;
- what we will do — and by when; and
- what you can do if you are not satisfied (§5 and §6).

### 3.5 Putting it right

Where we got it wrong, we will apologise, correct the error, and take steps to stop it recurring. Remedies may include:

- correcting or deleting personal data;
- correcting a billing error, or refunding or waiving a fee;
- restoring access;
- changing a process or a document;
- additional training or coaching for staff;
- a goodwill payment, **where appropriate and where it does not amount to compensation for trading losses** (§7).

## 4. Timeframes

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| **Acknowledgement** | **3 business days** |
| **Substantive response — general complaints** | **21 calendar days** from receipt |
| **If more time is needed** | We tell you why and give a revised date, and we update you at least every 21 days |
| **Final response — general complaints** | **8 weeks** at the outside |
| **Data-protection complaints (GDPR / UK GDPR)** | **1 month**, extendable by 2 further months for complex requests, with notice within the first month |
| **Data-protection complaints (US state laws)** | **45 days**, extendable by a further 45 days with notice; a refused request carries a right of **appeal**, decided within 45–60 days depending on the state |
| **Data-protection complaints (Vietnam, Decree 356/2025)** | Acknowledge in **2 working days**; complete within **10–20 days** depending on the request type |
| **Billing and payment disputes** | **10 business days** |

Where several deadlines apply, we apply the **shortest**.

---

## 5. If you are not satisfied with our response

5.1 **Ask for a review.** Reply to the final response within **30 days**, explaining why you remain dissatisfied. A person **more senior than the original decision-maker** — who was not involved in the first decision — will review it and respond within **21 days**.

5.2 **Escalate externally.** You may do this at any stage. You do not need our final response first, and you do not need our permission. See §6.

5.3 **Keep your evidence.** Keep copies of your correspondence, your complaint reference, and any transaction records.

## 7. What this procedure does not cover

7.1 **Trading losses.** Predict-A-Trade provides information and analytics, not advice, and does not execute or manage trades. **We do not compensate trading losses**, and no complaint outcome should be read as doing so. See the [Trading Risk Disclosure](https://predictatrade.com/legal/trading-risk-disclosure/) and [No Guarantee Notice](https://predictatrade.com/legal/no-guarantee/).

7.2 **Third parties.** Complaints about your broker, payment provider, data vendor or any other third party go to that party. We will help you identify who to contact.

7.3 **Unlawful, abusive or vexatious conduct.** We may limit or decline to continue correspondence that is threatening, abusive, discriminatory, or pursued persistently without substance. We will explain before we do so, and we will not use this clause to avoid a legitimate complaint.

7.4 **Statutory deadlines are not asserted without verified jurisdictional information.** Where a specific statutory time limit, escalation authority or compensation scheme applies to our activity, we say so. Where it does not, or where our jurisdictional position is unconfirmed, we do not claim one.

## 8. How we learn from complaints

8.1 We log every complaint with its reference, category, outcome and timeline.

8.2 We review complaint data at least **quarterly** to identify root causes and recurring themes, and annually at board or management level.

8.3 We report aggregate complaint volumes and themes in `[ANNUAL TRANSPARENCY REPORT URL]`, where we publish one.

8.4 Where a complaint reveals a systemic problem, we fix the system, not just the instance.
