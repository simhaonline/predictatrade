Canada

Law: Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), administered by the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (OPC); Quebec's Law 25 (formerly Bill 64), administered by the Commission d'accès à l'information (CAI); and substantially similar provincial laws in Alberta, British Columbia and elsewhere.

  • Rights of access, correction and withdrawal of consent, and the right to challenge compliance.
  • Quebec Law 25 adds data portability (from September 2024), the right to de-indexation / cessation of dissemination, transparency about automated decision-making (you must be informed and may ask for human intervention), mandatory privacy impact assessments for certain projects, a privacy officer by default, and breach notification to the CAI and affected individuals where a risk of serious injury exists.

Brazil

Law: Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD, Lei No. 13.709/2018), administered by the Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados (ANPD).

  • Ten legal bases, including consent, contract, legal obligation, and legitimate interest.
  • Rights: confirmation of processing · access · correction · anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data · portability · information about sharing · deletion where consent was the basis · withdrawal of consent · objection · review of automated decisions (Art. 20 — you may request a review of decisions made solely by automated processing that affect your interests, and we must provide an explanation of the criteria used).
  • DPO ("encarregado"): appointed and publicly identified — see §14.
  • Breach: notification to the ANPD and affected data subjects within a reasonable period, which the ANPD has indicated should be 3 business days ``.
  • Penalties: fines up to 2% of Brazilian revenue, capped at R$50 million per infraction, plus daily fines, publication of the infringement, and blocking or deletion of the data.
  • Consumer Defence Code (Law 8.078/1990) also applies, including the 7-day right of repentance for distance contracts.

Mexico

  • ARCO rights: Access, Rectification, Cancellation, Opposition. Requests are made directly to us; the Ministry handles enforcement and unresolved matters.

  • Processors are now directly regulated, not only controllers.
  • Penalties can reach substantial multiples of the minimum wage unit (UMA), doubled for sensitive data and repeat offences.

15.5 Conflicts between regimes

Where two laws apply, we apply the standard that gives you the stronger protection, unless doing so would breach the law of another applicable jurisdiction. Where a provision of this policy is unenforceable in your jurisdiction, the rest of the policy continues to apply.
