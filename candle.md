# 1) Bullish Walkthroughs

## A. Ultra Scalping Bullish Walkthrough

### Goal
Long entry using:
- **15m = bias**
- **5m = setup**
- **1m = entry**

## Step 1: Read the 15m Bias

Ask:
- Is price above **EMA 200**?
- Is **EMA 50 above EMA 100**?
- Is **EMA 9 above EMA 21**?
- Is SAR mostly below price?
- Are candles making higher highs / higher lows?

### What you see

### 15m Candle A
- Large green candle
- Closes above EMA 21 and EMA 50
- Price clearly above EMA 200
- Small upper wick
- EMA 9 above EMA 21

### Meaning
- Buyers are in control
- Trend is active
- Bias is **long only**

### 15m Candle B
- Small red candle
- Holds above EMA 21
- No aggressive bearish close

### Meaning
- This is only a pause or pullback
- Not a reversal

### Decision
Drop to the **5m chart** and wait for a healthy pullback.

---

## Step 2: Read the 5m Setup Candle by Candle

Your question:
> Is this pullback weak enough to buy?

### 5m Candle 1
- Red candle into EMA 21
- Moderate body
- Still above EMA 50
- No strong bearish breakdown

### Meaning
- Sellers are pulling price back
- But not breaking trend structure

### Action
- No entry yet

### 5m Candle 2
- Another red candle
- Smaller body than Candle 1
- Lower wick appears
- CCI still down but flattening
- StochRSI near low zone

### Meaning
- Pullback is continuing
- But with less force
- Buyers may be starting to defend the zone

### Action
- Stay patient

### 5m Candle 3
- Small hammer / doji type candle
- Dips under EMA 21 intrabar
- Closes back above EMA 21
- Leaves a clear lower wick
- Still above EMA 50

### Meaning
- Sellers tried lower prices and failed
- EMA zone is being defended
- Setup is now attractive

### Action
Move to the **1m chart**.

---

## Step 3: Read the 1m Entry Candle by Candle

### 1m Candle A
- Small red candle
- Tests below EMA 9 or EMA 21
- Lower wick appears
- Close not strong enough

### Meaning
- Selling pressure is slowing
- But no long trigger yet

### Action
- Wait

### 1m Candle B
- Small green candle
- Reclaims EMA 9
- Does not yet strongly break prior high

### Meaning
- Buyers are trying
- But still not enough proof

### Action
- Wait

### 1m Candle C
- Strong green candle
- Opens near EMA 9/21 zone
- Pushes up quickly
- Holds above midpoint during formation
- Breaks prior candle high
- Closes above EMA 9 and EMA 21
- Large body, small upper wick
- CCI turns positive
- StochRSI crosses up
- SAR flips bullish or is already bullish

### Meaning
This is the real bullish trigger.

### Action
- **Buy Stop above Candle C high**
- Or buy at close if that is your execution style

### 1m Candle D
- Opens and breaks Candle C high
- Triggers the order
- Holds above midpoint
- Does not fail back below EMA 21

### Meaning
- Follow-through is valid
- Buyers accepted higher prices

### Trade is active

### Trade Plan
- **SL**: below recent 1m swing low
- **TP1**: 1R
- **TP2**: 1.5R
- **TP3**: 2R or nearest 5m swing high

## B. Standard Swing Bullish Walkthrough

### Goal
Long entry using:
- **Daily = bias**
- **4H = setup**
- **1H = entry**

## Step 1: Read the Daily Bias

Ask:
- Is price above **EMA 200**?
- Is **EMA 50 above EMA 100**?
- Are candles making higher highs / higher lows?
- Is price respecting Daily EMA 21 / EMA 50?

### Daily Candle A
- Trend candle bullish
- Price above EMA 200
- EMA 50 above EMA 100
- Close above SMA 50 and SMA 100

### Meaning
- Main direction is up
- Only long setups preferred

### Daily Candle B
- Red pullback candle into Daily EMA 21
- Body not huge
- Lower wick appears

### Meaning
- Normal pullback inside bullish trend

### Daily Candle C
- Another small red or indecision candle
- Holds structure
- Stays above major swing low

### Meaning
- Trend is pausing, not reversing

### Decision
Drop to the **4H chart**.

---

## Step 2: Read the 4H Setup Candle by Candle

### 4H Candle 1
- Red candle into EMA 21
- Normal retracement

### Meaning
- First pullback leg only

### Action
- Wait

### 4H Candle 2
- Another red candle
- Smaller body than Candle 1
- Lower wick appears
- CCI still negative but flattening
- StochRSI low

### Meaning
- Sellers are still active
- But momentum is weakening

### Action
- Stay patient

### 4H Candle 3
- Dips toward EMA 50
- Long lower wick
- Closes in upper half of its range
- Does not collapse below EMA 50

### Meaning
- Test and reject candle
- Buyers defended the setup zone

### Action
- Setup now attractive

### 4H Candle 4
- Small green or inside candle
- Closes above prior candle midpoint/high
- CCI starts turning up

### Meaning
- Pullback may be ending
- Time to inspect the **1H chart**

---

## Step 3: Read the 1H Entry Candle by Candle

### 1H Candle A
- Small red candle
- Cannot continue downward strongly
- Lower wick visible

### Meaning
- Selling pressure remains weak

### Action
- Wait

### 1H Candle B
- Small green candle
- Reclaims EMA 9
- Still not decisive above EMA 21

### Meaning
- Early bullish attempt
- Not enough proof yet

### Action
- Wait

### 1H Candle C
- Strong bullish engulfing candle
- Opens near EMA 21
- Pushes above prior candle high
- Breaks minor structure high
- Closes above EMA 9 and EMA 21
- Large body, small upper wick
- CCI turns up
- StochRSI crosses up
- EMA 9 starts crossing above EMA 21

### Meaning
- First real momentum reclaim after the pullback
- Proper swing long trigger

### Action
- **Buy Stop above Candle C high**
- Or buy at close if allowed by your plan

### 1H Candle D
- Breaks Candle C high
- Holds above midpoint
- No immediate bearish rejection

### Meaning
- Follow-through confirms the entry

### Trade Plan
- **SL**: below 1H swing low or 4H pullback low
- **TP1**: 1R
- **TP2**: 2R
- **TP3**: 4R or nearest Daily swing high

# 3) Live Market Narration Style Examples

## A. Ultra Scalping Long — Live Narration

### 15m narration
- “Price is above EMA 200.”
- “EMA 50 is above EMA 100.”
- “The last strong candle was bullish.”
- “Current red candle is only pulling back, not reversing.”
- “Bias is long only.”

### 5m narration
- “First red 5m candle is pulling into EMA 21.”
- “No long yet. A pullback is not an entry.”
- “Second red 5m candle is smaller.”
- “Good. Pullback is losing energy.”
- “Now I see a lower wick near EMA 21.”
- “That tells me buyers are starting to defend.”
- “Still waiting. I want 1m to confirm momentum return.”

### 1m narration
- “First 1m candle is weak red. No entry.”
- “Second 1m candle turns green, but not strong enough. Still no entry.”
- “Now a strong green 1m candle forms.”
- “It is breaking the previous candle high.”
- “It is closing above EMA 9 and EMA 21.”
- “CCI is turning up and StochRSI is crossing up.”
- “Good. This is the first real reclaim by buyers.”
- “I do not buy in the middle of the candle.”
- “I wait for the close.”
- “The candle closes strong.”
- “Now I place a Buy Stop above that candle high.”
- “Next candle breaks the high and holds.”
- “Now I’m in.”

### Management narration
- “Stop goes below the recent 1m swing low.”
- “TP1 at 1R.”
- “At TP1 I move stop to breakeven.”
- “If momentum remains strong, hold for TP2 and TP3.”

# 4) Side-by-Side Bullish vs Bearish Comparison Sheet

## A. Bias Comparison

| Topic | Bullish Read | Bearish Read |
|---|---|---|
| EMA 200 | Price above EMA 200 | Price below EMA 200 |
| EMA 50 / 100 | EMA 50 above EMA 100 | EMA 50 below EMA 100 |
| EMA 9 / 21 | EMA 9 above EMA 21 | EMA 9 below EMA 21 |
| SAR | SAR mostly below price | SAR mostly above price |
| Structure | Higher highs / higher lows | Lower highs / lower lows |
| Preference | Look for longs only | Look for shorts only |

## B. Setup Comparison

| Topic | Bullish Setup | Bearish Setup |
|---|---|---|
| Pullback location | Into EMA 21 / EMA 50 support | Into EMA 21 / EMA 50 resistance |
| Opposite candles | Small red candles | Small green candles |
| Wick clue | Lower wicks appear | Upper wicks appear |
| Momentum behavior | Selling weakens | Buying weakens |
| CCI | Negative/flat then turns up | Positive/flat then turns down |
| StochRSI | Low/reset then turns up | High/reset then turns down |

## C. Trigger Candle Comparison

| Topic | Bullish Trigger | Bearish Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Candle body | Strong green body | Strong red body |
| Close position | Closes near high | Closes near low |
| EMA relation | Close above EMA 9 and EMA 21 | Close below EMA 9 and EMA 21 |
| Structure break | Breaks previous candle high | Breaks previous candle low |
| Wick quality | Small upper wick | Small lower wick |
| Meaning | Buyers regained control | Sellers regained control |

## D. Follow-Through Comparison

| Topic | Bullish | Bearish |
|---|---|---|
| Next candle | Breaks trigger high and holds | Breaks trigger low and holds |
| Warning sign | Immediate dump back below EMA 21 | Immediate squeeze back above EMA 21 |
| Better entry | First strong reclaim candle | First strong rejection / breakdown candle |
| Lower-quality entry | 3rd/4th extended green candle | 3rd/4th extended red candle |

## E. Management Comparison

| Topic | Bullish | Bearish |
|---|---|---|
| Stop placement | Below swing low | Above swing high |
| TP1 | 1R | 1R |
| TP2 | Strategy dependent | Strategy dependent |
| TP3 | Strategy dependent / trail | Strategy dependent / trail |
| Move to BE | After TP1 | After TP1 |

# 5) Printable Candle-by-Candle Decision Tree

Use this for any strategy.

## Step 1: Bias Check

### If price is above EMA 200 and EMA 50 > EMA 100
- Bias = **Bullish**
- Continue to Step 2A

### If price is below EMA 200 and EMA 50 < EMA 100
- Bias = **Bearish**
- Continue to Step 2B

### If price is around EMA 200 or MAs are flat/tangled
- **Cancel / No trade**

---

## Step 2A: Bullish Setup Check

### If price pulls back into EMA 21 or EMA 50
- Continue

### If pullback candles are small and weakening
- Continue

### If lower wicks start appearing near support
- Continue

### If one large bearish candle breaks structure strongly
- **Cancel / Wait for new setup**

### If price is far above EMA 21/50 and already extended
- **Wait, do not chase**

Go to Step 3A.

---

## Step 2B: Bearish Setup Check

### If price bounces into EMA 21 or EMA 50
- Continue

### If bounce candles are small and weakening
- Continue

### If upper wicks start appearing near resistance
- Continue

### If one large bullish candle breaks structure strongly
- **Cancel / Wait for new setup**

### If price is far below EMA 21/50 and already extended
- **Wait, do not chase**

Go to Step 3B.

---

## Step 3A: Bullish Trigger Candle Decision

### If the live candle:
- reclaims EMA 9 / EMA 21
- breaks previous candle high
- holds above midpoint
- keeps a strong green body

Then:
- **Wait for close**

### If the candle closes:
- above EMA 9
- above EMA 21
- above previous candle high
- near its high

Then:
- **Enter / place Buy Stop**

### If the candle:
- leaves a long upper wick
- closes back under EMA 21
- fails to break structure

Then:
- **Cancel / wait for next candle**

---

## Step 3B: Bearish Trigger Candle Decision

### If the live candle:
- rejects EMA 9 / EMA 21
- breaks previous candle low
- holds below midpoint
- keeps a strong red body

Then:
- **Wait for close**

### If the candle closes:
- below EMA 9
- below EMA 21
- below previous candle low
- near its low

Then:
- **Enter / place Sell Stop**

### If the candle:
- leaves a long lower wick
- closes back above EMA 21
- fails to break structure

Then:
- **Cancel / wait for next candle**

---

## Step 4: Confirmation Score Check

### Bullish
Add 1 point each if:
- EMA 9 > EMA 21
- SAR bullish
- CCI > 0 and rising
- StochRSI turning up

### Bearish
Add 1 point each if:
- EMA 9 < EMA 21
- SAR bearish
- CCI < 0 and falling
- StochRSI turning down

### If score is below minimum required
- **Wait / Cancel**

### If score meets minimum
- Continue to Step 5

---

## Step 5: Room-to-Target Check

### If nearest support/resistance blocks TP2
- **Cancel**

### If there is enough room to TP2
- Continue

---

## Step 6: Entry Follow-Through Check

### After trigger candle closes:
- place stop order beyond trigger candle

### If next candle triggers the order and holds direction
- **Trade active**

### If next candle immediately reverses hard through EMA 21
- **Warning / reduce confidence**

### If pending order is not triggered within allowed bars
- **Cancel order**

# 7) Final Principle

> **Bias gives permission.**  
> **Setup gives location.**  
> **Realtime candles show intent.**  
> **Closed candles confirm entry.**  
> **Indicators support the decision; they should not overrule price.**
