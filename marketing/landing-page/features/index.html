Predict-A-Trade / Capabilities

Context before the decision.

Predict-A-Trade brings multiple forms of market context together to make XAUUSD analysis easier to inspect, explain, and use responsibly.

The public product focuses on explainable market intelligence—not promises, guaranteed outcomes, or a substitute for independent judgement.

Technical indicators

A balanced view of trend, momentum, volatility, and session context.

  • EMA and SMA trend references
  • MACD, RSI, ADX, and directional movement
  • ATR and Bollinger Bands for volatility context
  • Stochastic, CCI, VWAP, and supporting indicators

Market structure

Price behaviour is considered through visible structural changes.

  • Swing highs and swing lows
  • Break of Structure and Change of Character
  • Trend, range, and breakout context
  • Fibonacci and pivot references where relevant

Liquidity and price zones

Potential areas of interest are mapped as part of the broader market picture.

  • Liquidity pools and equal highs or lows
  • Liquidity sweep and reclaim context
  • Fair Value Gaps
  • Order Blocks and Breaker Blocks

Multi-timeframe alignment

Shorter-term observations are viewed alongside higher-timeframe context.

  • M1, M5, M15, M30, H1, and H4 analysis
  • D1 and W1 context for broader direction
  • Bullish, bearish, or neutral alignment
  • Context before lower-timeframe interpretation

Sessions and market regime

Time of day and changing market conditions add important context.

  • Tokyo, London, overlap, and New York sessions
  • UTC-aware session interpretation
  • Trending, ranging, breakout, and mean-reversion context
  • Volatility and unstable-market awareness

Readable decision support

Signals are presented as analytical evidence, not as certainty.

  • Evidence-based market summaries
  • Probability treated as probability—not fact
  • Clear WAIT and NO-TRADE context where appropriate
  • Public explanations designed for informed review

Designed for responsible interpretation

Market data can be delayed, incomplete, or different between providers. Volatility, liquidity, leverage, spreads, slippage, execution conditions, news, and unexpected events can change outcomes. Predict-A-Trade does not provide personalised financial advice or guarantee profits, accuracy, or results.
