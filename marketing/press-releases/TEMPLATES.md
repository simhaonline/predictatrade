## Press Release Guidelines

### Before Issuing

- [ ] All facts verified
- [ ] Legal review completed
- [ ] Risk disclaimers included
- [ ] Quotes approved by quoted individuals
- [ ] Contact information accurate
- [ ] embargo dates respected

### Distribution

- [ ] Wire service selected (if applicable)
- [ ] Media list updated
- [ ] Social media posts prepared
- [ ] Website updated (if applicable)
- [ ] Internal communications sent

### Follow-Up

- [ ] Media inquiries tracked
- [ ] Coverage monitored
- [ ] Social media engagement tracked
- [ ] Results documented

### Legal Requirements

- [ ] No misleading claims
- [ ] Risk warnings prominent
- [ ] Testimonials with disclaimers
- [ ] No guaranteed returns
- [ ] Regulatory compliance verified

### Tone

- Formal but accessible
- Factual, not promotional
- Evidence-based
- Transparent about limitations

### Length

- Headline: 10-15 words
- Subheadline: 20-30 words
- Body: 300-500 words
- Boilerplate: 50-100 words
