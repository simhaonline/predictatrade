## WhatsApp Best Practices

### Content Guidelines

**DO:**
• Provide value in every message
• Keep messages concise (WhatsApp is for quick communication)
• Use formatting (emojis, bold, bullet points) for readability
• Respond promptly to support inquiries
• Include disclaimers on trading-related content

**DON'T:**
• Send too many messages (max 2-3 per week for broadcasts)
• Promise guaranteed profits
• Share specific trade signals without disclaimers
• Ignore support inquiries
• Use excessive capitalization or punctuation

### Posting Schedule

**Weekly:**
• 1 market update
• 1 educational tip
• 1 risk management reminder (rotate)

**Monthly:**
• 1 platform update
• 1 community spotlight
• 1 disclaimer post

### Disclaimer Placement

**Required on:**
• All trading-related broadcasts
• All market commentary
• All performance discussions

**Format:**
> ⚠️ Trading involves risk. Past performance ≠ future results.

### Customer Support

**Response Time:** Within 24 hours (preferably faster)

**Escalation:**
• Technical issues → Support team
• Compliance concerns → Legal team
• Abuse/harassment → Remove from group

**Tone:** Helpful, professional, solution-oriented
