Four Strategies for Different Styles

Not all traders are the same. Choose what fits YOUR approach.

Standard Scalping

  • Quick entries and exits
  • Higher frequency
  • Smaller targets
  • Best for active traders

⚠️ Requires frequent monitoring

Ultra Scalping

  • Very short-term moves
  • Requires tight spreads
  • Highest frequency
  • Best for experienced traders

⚠️ Not for beginners

Standard Swing

  • Medium-term moves
  • Fewer trades
  • Larger targets
  • Best for busy traders

⚠️ Requires patience

Trend Swing

  • Rides established trends
  • Widest stops
  • Targets large moves
  • Best for patient traders

⚠️ Can handle drawdowns
