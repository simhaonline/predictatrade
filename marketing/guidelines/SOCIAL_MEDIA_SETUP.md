# Social Media Account Setup Guide

## Predict-A-Trade

This guide provides step-by-step instructions for setting up social media accounts for Predict-A-Trade.

---

## 1. Instagram

### Account Setup
1. Download Instagram app or go to instagram.com
2. Click "Sign Up"
3. Enter email: **marketing@predictatrade.com**
4. Choose username: **@predictatrade** or **@predict.atrade**
5. Complete profile:
   - Profile Photo: Use logo from brand kit
   - Bio: "AI-Powered XAUUSD Trading Signals | Risk-Managed | Demo Available"
   - Website: https://predictatrade.com
   - Category: Financial Service

### First Post
- Welcome post with logo
- Use 10-15 relevant hashtags
- Pin top 3 posts: Features, Risk Disclaimer, Demo CTA

### Content Strategy
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts/week
- **Best Times:** 9am, 12pm, 5pm (EST)
- **Content Mix:** 40% educational, 30% testimonials, 20% product features, 10% promotions
