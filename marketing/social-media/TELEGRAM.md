## Telegram Best Practices

### Content Guidelines

**DO:**
• Provide educational value
• Be transparent about results (wins AND losses)
• Include risk disclaimers on all trading-related content
• Respond to community questions promptly
• Share market analysis (not recommendations)

**DON'T:**
• Promise guaranteed profits
• Share specific buy/sell signals without disclaimers
• Delete losing trade posts
• Make unrealistic claims
• Ignore community concerns

### Posting Schedule

**Daily:**
• Morning market update
• Evening summary (if significant events)

**Weekly:**
• Performance report
• Educational post
• Q&A session

**Monthly:**
• Platform updates
• Strategy reviews
• Community spotlight

### Disclaimer Placement

**Required on:**
• All trading-related posts
• All performance reports
• All signal posts
• All market commentary

**Format:**
> ⚠️ Risk Disclaimer: [Standard risk warning]

### Community Management

**Response Time:** Within 24 hours (preferably faster)

**Escalation:**
• Technical issues → Support team
• Compliance concerns → Legal team
• Abuse/harassment → Ban immediately

**Tone:** Helpful, professional, educational
